1 Introduction
Softwaresystems,fromcloudservicesandmicroservicestonetworkedandcyber-physicalinfrastructures,emit
massivevolumesofexecutionlogsthatrecordruntimestates,events,andfailures.Logsareaprimarysourceof
evidenceforreliabilityengineeringandoperations:theysupportdebugging,monitoring,incidenttriage,and
postmortemanalysis[36,192,219].However,modernlogstreamsarenotoriouslydifficulttoanalyzeatscale.They
aresemi-structured (mixingfree-formtextwithidentifiersandcode-liketokens),high-volume/high-velocity,and
continuouslyevolvingduetosoftwareupdates,configurationchanges,anddeploymentdiversity.Consequently,
manualinspectionandkeywordsearchremaincommonyetbrittle,whileautomatedpipelinesoftensufferfrom
formatdrift,long-tailbehaviors,limitedlabeleddata,andinconsistentloggingquality[21,46,47,87,139].
